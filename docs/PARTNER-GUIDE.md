# Split Ledger — guide for the two of you

Split Ledger works out who owes whom each month for the purchases you share. Each of you
works in your own browser on your own laptop. Your full bank history stays on your laptop;
only the purchases you mark as shared, with date, merchant, category, amount, and share,
are sent to the other person.

## Once, at the start (about 5 minutes)

1. Open the invitation email from Split Ledger (sent through Clerk, the sign-in service)
   and sign up with that same email address.
2. On the page that asks you to choose a household, choose the household you were invited
   to. Do not create a new one.
3. Type a short alias (a name without an email address) and choose **Join as partner**.
   Both screens then show **2 of 2 connected**.

## Every month (about 15–20 minutes each)

1. Open Split Ledger. It opens the month that just ended.
2. **Import**: for each card, copy the transaction table from your bank's website and paste
   it into **Statement text**, choose the bank and card, then **Parse and add**.
3. **Review**: choose how each purchase is shared. **Mine** is yours alone, **Theirs** is
   the other person's, and 70/30, 50/50, 30/70 split it. Switch to **Merchant** grouping and
   use **Apply to group** to decide every purchase from a merchant at once. **✦ Remember**
   makes a merchant's split automatic next month.
4. **Share / Status**: choose **Submit shared projection**. Then use **Copy message** (or
   **Share** or **Email**) to tell the other person it is their turn.
5. The coordinator opens **Settle**, checks the result, and closes the month. Paying each
   other happens outside the app, by bank transfer.

## Good to know

- Changing the month never erases anything; each month keeps its own draft.
- If you change something after submitting, choose **Replace submission**.
- After a month closes, Settle offers **Download private session**: keep that file
  somewhere private. It protects your remembered splits if the browser is cleared.
- Something looks wrong? Open **Rules & Files** → **Run self-test**, then **Copy safe
  diagnostic report**, and send that report. It contains no names, months, merchants, or
  amounts.
- Never paste bank or card numbers anywhere other than the statement box, and never share
  the private session file.
