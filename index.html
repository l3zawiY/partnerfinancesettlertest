Who and which cards

Auto-default small unknowns

Transactions below this amount with no matching rule get assigned automatically. Leave this at 0 for your first month. Half your transactions are under $25 but they're only about a sixth of the money, and they repeat — so tag them once, let them become rules, then raise the threshold. Setting it high in month one bakes in a bias you'd never see.
